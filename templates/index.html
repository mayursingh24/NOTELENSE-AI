About NoteLense

NoteLense — Zoom into what matters

NoteLense is an AI-powered Notes Analyzer built using Python, Flask and Google Gemini AI. It transforms ordinary notes into structured study material including summaries, revision notes, important questions, MCQs, viva questions, flashcards and personalized study plans. Designed especially for students to save time and improve exam preparation.
